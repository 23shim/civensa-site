# Civensa editorial research register

Checked: 12 August 2026

## Method

The ten authority articles use official guidance for legal rules and current dates. Reports, books and podcast notes provide context. Each article links its evidence at section level and lists every source at the end.

The editorial process has three stages:

1. Draft around a distinct supplier decision and named evidence.
2. Humanizer review for rhythm, specificity, false certainty and invented facts.
3. No-AI-slop review for throat-clearing, binary contrasts, vague attribution, promotional language, repeated structures and banned vocabulary.

Current opportunity dates are labelled with the checked date. They must be reviewed against the live agreement page and Find a Tender before publication in another channel.
