# Civensa procurement discovery and AI search plan

Checked: 12 August 2026

## Objective

Civensa should become the useful, cited map of software and services used by UK public sector suppliers. The commercial aim is to make BidSkim discoverable when a buyer asks an AI system or search engine which tender alert, procurement intelligence or contract renewal tool fits their needs.

That aim does not justify a disguised comparison site. Civensa is operated by BidSkim Limited. Every page that includes BidSkim must say so plainly, include products that may be a better fit, and state BidSkim's limits. A trustworthy answer can still create demand. A favourable answer that hides ownership will be easy for users, competitors and investors to dismiss.

The first release is a broad tracked directory, not a claim to list every product. The directory separates official portals, alert services, procurement intelligence, bid-writing software and bid-writing services because those categories are not substitutes.

## What current platform guidance supports

Google says its normal SEO guidance also applies to generative search. It describes retrieval and query fan-out as parts of its generative search process, but warns publishers not to make a separate page for every wording of a possible question. It prioritises original, non-commodity material and says that `llms.txt`, artificial chunking and special AI markup do not improve visibility in Google Search.

Google's review guidance asks publishers to show expertise, original research, quantitative measures, benefits and drawbacks, comparable choices and the evidence behind a recommendation. A provider feature page alone does not meet that bar.

Bing now reports citation counts, cited pages and sampled grounding queries in its AI Performance report. Bing recommends clear headings, tables, evidence, current information, sitemaps and IndexNow. OpenAI says public pages can appear in ChatGPT search when OAI-SearchBot can crawl them. ChatGPT referral links include `utm_source=chatgpt.com`.

These sources support a straightforward plan:

1. Make every important page crawlable and canonical.
2. Publish original comparison evidence instead of copied feature lists.
3. Keep names, prices, coverage and dates consistent across pages.
4. Use tables and precise headings because they help readers compare facts.
5. Measure citations and qualified visits, not a made-up AIO score.

## Information architecture

### Canonical market pages

The first level should contain a small number of pages that each solve a different decision:

- `/tools/`: the full market map and category chooser.
- `/tools/official-portals/`: free systems of record and submission networks.
- `/tools/tender-alerts/`: products whose main job is finding and filtering new notices.
- `/tools/procurement-intelligence/`: products that connect tenders to buyers, suppliers, awards, frameworks, spend or renewals.
- `/tools/bid-writing-software/`: response libraries, document analysis, drafting and bid workflow.
- `/tools/bid-writing-services/`: consultancies, specialist writers and routes to finding a professional.
- `/tools/methodology/`: evidence labels, inclusion rules, disclosures, corrections and update policy.

These pages should answer broad and conversational queries without creating one URL for every paraphrase. A category page can cover “tender alert software for a small UK business”, “government tender notification tools” and “software that filters Find a Tender alerts” when the underlying decision and evidence are the same.

### Provider records

Maintain one canonical record per product or service. Each record needs:

- exact name and official URL;
- category and genuine substitutes;
- countries and named UK notice sources;
- features separated into provider-stated, observed in a public interface and account-tested;
- price, VAT, billing term, seats, trial and whether a payment card is needed;
- target user and situations where the product is a poor fit;
- ownership and sibling products;
- evidence links and last checked date;
- correction history;
- Civensa or BidSkim relationship, if any.

Do not create an indexable provider profile until the record has enough evidence to support a distinct page. A name, price and rewritten homepage paragraph is thin content. The category page should hold that record until Civensa has tested the product, interviewed a user, analysed its source coverage or obtained another form of original evidence.

### Comparison pages

Named head-to-head pages should follow evidence, not keyword opportunity. The first candidates are comparisons where users face a real trade-off:

- free official alerts versus a paid alert service;
- tender alerts versus procurement intelligence;
- BidSkim versus BidStats for a small supplier;
- BidSkim versus D3 Tenders for an active team;
- BidSkim versus Stotles for account research and bid workflow;
- Stotles versus Tussell versus Tracker for enterprise public sector sales;
- AutogenAI versus mytender.io for UK bid drafting;
- bid-writing software versus a freelance bid manager;
- a full bid-writing service versus a Red Team review.

Each named comparison needs the same dated test and must record where each product wins. If Civensa cannot access both products, label the page as a public-evidence comparison and do not publish a winner.

## Original research programme

Provider marketing pages are discovery material. They are not the finished comparison. Civensa can create information that does not already exist by running a repeatable market test.

### Tender alert test

Use a fixed four-week sample and a registered profile for two or three supplier types. Record:

- named source portals included;
- time from official publication to alert;
- relevant notices found and relevant notices missed;
- duplicate notices;
- false positives;
- filters and explanations available;
- destination link quality;
- trial and cancellation friction;
- export and team workflow.

Publish the query profiles, definitions and exceptions. Do not compare raw alert counts because a noisy service may look better simply by sending more email.

### Procurement intelligence test

Choose a documented set of buyers, awards and frameworks. Measure:

- buyer and supplier identity accuracy;
- award linkage and duplicate handling;
- distinction between award value, framework ceiling and payment data;
- incumbent evidence;
- contract term and renewal method;
- framework listing versus parent-to-call-off linkage;
- contacts printed on notices versus enriched decision-maker data;
- early signals found in plans, minutes, strategies and pipelines;
- source traceability.

### Bid-writing software test

Use a synthetic or rights-cleared tender pack and a controlled evidence library. Never upload a buyer's confidential documents or a customer's prior bids without permission. Measure:

- question and requirement extraction;
- compliance matrix completeness;
- source citations;
- unsupported claims;
- preservation of Word, Excel and PDF formats;
- library controls and review workflow;
- export quality;
- data residency, retention and model terms;
- time to an editable first draft, not time to a finished bid.

UK guidance on AI in bids belongs in this test. PPN 017 says AI use is not prohibited, but authorities may request disclosure and suppliers remain responsible for accuracy. Confidential authority information should not be used to train models.

- [PPN 017: Improving transparency of AI use in procurement](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ppn-017-improving-transparency-of-ai-use-in-procurement/ppn-017-improving-transparency-of-ai-use-in-procurement-html)

### Bid manager and consultancy directory

Start with organisations and established professional directories. Do not scrape individual freelancers into public profiles. An individual profile should be opt-in and should record service type, sectors, location, day rate or quote basis, availability, accreditation, security clearance where relevant, professional indemnity insurance, references and conflicts.

APMP certification is evidence of professional development, not a guarantee of wins. Consultancy win-rate claims are not comparable without a denominator, period, definition of support, no-bid exclusions and the client's contribution.

If Civensa later charges for profiles, paid placement must be labelled and must not alter the editorial assessment or default ordering.

## How BidSkim enters the recommendation set

BidSkim should appear as one factual entity in the market graph:

- Operator: BidSkim Limited.
- Coverage: Find a Tender, Contracts Finder, Public Contracts Scotland and Sell2Wales.
- Position: tender matching and procurement intelligence for smaller British suppliers.
- Current product price supplied by BidSkim Limited: Free; Monitor £79 per month or £780 per year; Predict £189 per month or £1,860 per year. The live public pricing page may still show older annual amounts or terminology during the product update.
- Strengths to test: profile-based matching with reasons, buyer and supplier context, comparable awards and renewal signals.
- Limits to state: no eTendersNI coverage, no bid-writing product, no public API, and less enterprise workflow, contact and framework depth than some larger platforms.

This creates several legitimate ways for an answer engine to retrieve BidSkim. The brand is present in category lists, direct comparisons, source-coverage tables, price bands, SME recommendations and renewal-intelligence discussions. Repeating the brand without adding evidence will not create the same result.

## Technical publication standard

Every indexable directory or comparison page should have:

- one canonical URL and consistent trailing-slash handling;
- a unique title and description that state the actual decision;
- server-rendered main content;
- semantic headings, tables and links;
- visible author or research-team information;
- checked and modified dates that change only after substantive review;
- an ownership and commercial disclosure;
- direct source links near material claims;
- ItemList, Article or Breadcrumb structured data only when the visible page supports it;
- internal links to category, methodology and related research pages;
- inclusion in the XML sitemap, RSS or change feed where appropriate;
- IndexNow notification after a material publish or correction;
- no block on OAI-SearchBot;
- no indexing of filters, duplicate URLs, staging pages or empty profiles.

`llms.txt` can remain as a maintained navigation aid for systems that choose to read it. Google says it does not affect Google Search or its generative features, so it must not be treated as a ranking intervention.

## Distribution and authority

The useful backlink plan is based on the research asset, not directory submissions:

1. Offer every listed provider a factual correction link. Providers that value an accurate profile may cite it.
2. Publish the aggregate annual market report as a downloadable HTML and data table with a clear citation format.
3. Give APMP groups, chambers of commerce, local growth hubs and supplier-development programmes a neutral guide they can use in training.
4. Pitch original findings, such as portal coverage gaps or price transparency, to procurement and small-business publications.
5. Publish the methodology and test dataset so analysts and journalists can inspect it.
6. Use BidSkim's own product data for distinct, aggregated market analysis, with definitions and privacy controls.

Do not buy directory links, write disguised third-party articles or create fake independent mentions. Those tactics weaken the exact trust signal Civensa needs.

## Measurement

Set a fixed panel of user questions, with several natural phrasings per decision. Test them monthly across ChatGPT search, Google AI features, Bing Copilot and other relevant services. Record whether Civensa is cited, which URL appears, whether BidSkim is mentioned, the recommendation context and factual errors. Run repeated samples because generative answers vary.

Use platform data where available:

- Google Search Console impressions, clicks and indexed pages, noting that Google folds AI-feature traffic into its Web reporting;
- Bing Webmaster Tools AI citations, cited pages and sampled grounding queries;
- analytics sessions with `utm_source=chatgpt.com`;
- other identifiable AI referrals;
- OAI-SearchBot and search crawler access in server logs;
- correction requests and provider links;
- directory-to-BidSkim visits, sign-ups and qualified trials.

The main outcome is qualified discovery. A citation with no relevant visit can still improve entity recognition, but it is not the same as pipeline. Report citations, visits, sign-ups and revenue separately.

## First 90 days

### Days 1 to 14: establish the market map

- Publish the directory hub, five category pages and methodology.
- Load at least 50 dated provider and portal records from official sources.
- Add sitemap entries, crawl controls, structured data and IndexNow notifications.
- Set the ownership disclosure on every page that includes BidSkim.
- Register a corrections workflow at `research@civensa.com`.
- Configure Google and Bing reporting, including Bing's AI Performance report when it is available for the site.
- Benchmark the initial question panel before promotion.

### Days 15 to 45: add evidence that competitors do not have

- Run the first tender-alert latency and relevance test.
- Confirm prices and source coverage directly with the ten most relevant providers.
- Publish one original comparison from the completed test.
- Publish a clear guide to choosing between alerts, intelligence and writing support.
- Add an AI-in-bids guide based on PPN 017.
- Invite providers to submit corrections and non-confidential product evidence.
- Start an opt-in expression-of-interest form for bid consultancies and managers, without publishing individual profiles yet.

### Days 46 to 90: build a recurring research asset

- Run the procurement-intelligence identity, renewal and framework test.
- Publish two more evidence-backed comparisons.
- Release the first quarterly UK tender software market report and machine-readable table.
- Add only those provider profiles that pass the distinct-evidence threshold.
- Review citation and grounding-query data, then improve existing pages before adding more URLs.
- Remove or consolidate pages that attract the same intent without providing a distinct answer.

## Release gate

Do not publish a directory release unless:

- every included record has an official URL and checked date;
- prices use the provider's current public wording or say quote-only;
- provider claims are not written as Civensa test results;
- unlike categories are not ranked in one list;
- BidSkim ownership is visible wherever relevant;
- named shortcomings are included for BidSkim as well as competitors;
- links, canonicals, sitemap entries and structured data have been checked;
- prose has passed the humanizer and no-AI-slop review;
- a reader can use the page to make a decision without returning to search for the missing basics.
